What we serve

The full menu

Everything Frenchies makes — from a classic scoop to a Rhode Island frozen lemonade. Call ahead or stop in at 995 Mineral Spring Ave, North Providence.

Hard Serve Ice Cream

Dense, rich, scooped to order.

Call for pricing

Some days you want the real thing — a proper scoop of hard ice cream in a cup or a cone. At Frenchies, it's made to be exactly that: cold, dense, and flavorful in a way that a soft-serve machine can't replicate.

Whether you're in the mood for classic vanilla, deep chocolate, or something fruit-forward, hard serve is what you reach for when soft serve won't cut it. Ask at the window for today's selections.

Soft Serve

Swirled fresh. Light and smooth.

Call for pricing

There's a reason soft serve has been the default summer stop for generations in Rhode Island — it's immediate, it's satisfying, and when it's made right, it tastes clean and light.

Frenchies does vanilla, chocolate, and the classic twist. Comes in a cup or cone. Gets handed to you fast so nothing melts on the way to your car.

Sundaes

Ice cream with room to build.

Call for pricing

A sundae is the order you place when you're not in a hurry. You pick the ice cream, you pick the toppings, and you get something that's actually built around what you want — not what's easiest to make.

Frenchies offers sundaes with hot fudge, strawberries, whipped cream, nuts, and more. If you haven't had one in a while, this is the reminder to order one.

Milkshakes

Blended with real ice cream.

Call for pricing

A milkshake that's mostly air isn't a milkshake. Frenchies makes them thick, blended with real hard ice cream so the flavor is actually there from the first sip to the last.

Vanilla, chocolate, and strawberry are the standbys. Ask if you want something different — the point is you're not just getting a cold glass of milk with flavor syrup.

Frozen Lemonade

The Rhode Island standard.

Call for pricing

Frozen lemonade is a Rhode Island thing. Not a slushie, not a smoothie — frozen lemonade. Tart, icy, and satisfying in a way that cold water on a hot day just isn't.

On a July afternoon at 3pm, this is the thing. Frenchies makes it the right way, which is why people drive to Mineral Spring Ave specifically for it.

Non-Dairy Options

Real flavor. No dairy.

Call for pricing

If dairy isn't an option for you, Frenchies has non-dairy selections that hold up on their own. The goal is a scoop that tastes like ice cream, not like a health product.

Ask at the window what's available — the selection may vary by day and season.

No Sugar Added

Watching your sugar intake, not your enjoyment.

Call for pricing

Managing blood sugar doesn't mean skipping ice cream. Frenchies carries no-sugar-added options that actually taste like something worth ordering.

These aren't afterthoughts tacked onto the menu. Ask for the current options when you visit.

Frozen Yogurt

Lighter and tangier.

Call for pricing

Frozen yogurt is for the days you want something cold and satisfying but a little lighter. It has a distinct tang that ice cream doesn't — some people prefer it, especially in the summer heat.

Frenchies makes it with the same attention as the rest of the menu. Toppings available on request.

Pup Cups

Because your dog waited in the car.

Ask at the window

A small cup of plain soft serve, sized for a dog who's been very patient in the back seat. No toppings, no sugar — just plain vanilla soft serve that most dogs consume in roughly four seconds.

It's free or nominal. Ask at the window. Bring the dog.

Gluten Free

The whole family can order.

Call for pricing

Gluten-free cone options are available for guests who need them. Not every flavor and option is guaranteed gluten-free due to shared equipment, so ask at the window about today's safe selections.

The staff can walk you through what works for you.
