About the shop

A corner of Mineral Spring Ave that feels like summer.

Frenchies has been serving the North Providence community from 995 Mineral Spring Ave. It's the kind of place people bring their kids on a Tuesday for no reason — because some days, ice cream is the whole point.

Hard serve, soft serve, sundaes, milkshakes, frozen lemonade, yogurt, pup cups for your dog — the menu has grown with the neighborhood. Non-dairy and no-sugar-added options are available because everyone deserves to walk away with something good.

Over 205 reviews, a 4.1-star average, and regulars who show up every week. That's not advertising — that's what happens when a shop takes care of its neighbors.
